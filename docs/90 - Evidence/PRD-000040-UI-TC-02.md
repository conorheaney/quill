## Preconditions

- Packaged Quill `1.0.16` Windows candidate was available.
- Quill was launched with the custom HTML title bar visible.
- Manual verification was performed on the Windows desktop.
- Display scaling was not recorded.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Click **Minimize** and restore Quill from the taskbar.
2. Click **Maximize/Restore** to maximize the window, then click it again to restore the previous size.
3. Double-click an empty area of the title bar from restored and maximized states.
4. With no unsaved changes, click **Close**.
5. Relaunch Quill, make an identifiable edit, and click **Close**.
6. Cancel the close confirmation and verify that Quill remains open with the edit preserved.
7. Close again and confirm the close/discard action.

## Expected Results

Minimize, restore, maximize, title-bar double-click, clean close, unsaved-change confirmation, cancellation, and confirmed close all behave as expected.
