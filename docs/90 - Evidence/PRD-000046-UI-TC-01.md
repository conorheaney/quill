## Preconditions

- Use the committed packaged Quill 1.1.2 candidate.
- Close any other Quill instance that could interfere with startup observation.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Launch the packaged Quill application.
2. Observe the startup surface while initialization is in progress.
3. Confirm the branded quill or pen writing animation is visible on the blank page.
4. Attempt to access the application's other controls while the startup animation is displayed.

## Expected Results

- A branded startup animation shows a quill or pen writing on a blank page.
- The application's other controls cannot be accessed while the startup animation is displayed.
